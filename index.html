Rido: new app flow (v1.6.1)

Rido moves from a cab comparison app to a connected travel app. Cabs keep the compare-and-redirect model. Flights, hotels and intercity buses are searched, paid for and confirmed inside Rido through TBO. My Trips, payments, membership, loyalty and notifications are shared across every service. What changed in v1.6.1 (scope update)
  • Voice landing takes the whole trip in one sentence (origin, destination, duration, budget and services) and fills the fields. One follow-up question is asked only when dates are essential.
  • Smart service choice is based on the full requirement, not the trip type: flights, hotels, a bus alternative and cabs are activated for the journey.
  • Recommendations combine flight, hotel, transfers and cabs into budget options with a one-sentence reason. Alternatives appear when direct flights exceed the admin threshold, and flexible dates are searched per date (±3 days).
  • AI trip planner builds a three-day connected plan for Jaipur → Mumbai, with estimated expenditure against the budget, a booking summary, and approval for changes to confirmed bookings.
  • Honest status: flights, hotels and buses are confirmed only after the provider returns confirmation. Cabs are never shown as booked without it.
Rules that shape every screen
  • Live prices only. Every fare comes from TBO or the cab connector, and is re-checked before payment.
  • AI never invents prices. AI understands, plans and explains. Estimates are labelled as estimates.
  • Cabs are never “booked” by Rido. The ride is booked in Uber, Ola or Rapido. Rido confirms only when the user says so.
  • One checkout. The same Rido payment gateway serves flights, hotels, buses and memberships.
  • No hidden fees. The price shown is the price paid. Rido aims for no separate convenience fee.
  • Helpful, not pushy. Reminders only when relevant to the trip, with one-tap actions and opt-out.
